Privacy Policy — English

11 November 2026 · PrizivoCheck · com.velqenor.prizivocheck

This Privacy Policy explains how the PrizivoCheck Android application handles data. The app compares product prices per unit using information you enter. Its main comparison functions work offline. This policy covers the app, not the privacy practices of a store or service you independently choose to use.

Who is responsible and how to contact us

App: PrizivoCheck (Android package com.velqenor.prizivocheck). The responsible developer is the publisher identified on PrizivoCheck's official Google Play listing. Privacy contact: support.velqenor@gmail.com. No account registration is required to use the app.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the product names, unit prices, quantities, measurement units, currencies, promotions, recent comparisons, favorite templates, manually recorded prices, and settings you choose to enter or save. It uses local SQLite storage and app preferences. These details can include information you voluntarily type, so avoid entering information you do not wish to store.

We do not run an app server that automatically receives your product lists, shopping prices, comparisons, or locally saved history. The calculations take place on your device.

Why the app uses these details

The data you provide is used to calculate comparable unit prices and savings, display results, keep your chosen history and templates, and remember settings such as language and theme. Where applicable, processing is necessary to provide the app features you request; any support emails are handled to respond to inquiries and protect our legitimate interests in supporting the app, subject to applicable law.

Sharing, imports, exports and backups

Only when you ask the app to share or export results, CSV files, or JSON backups does it invoke Android's share sheet. You select the destination or other app. Once you send or save a file elsewhere, that destination's provider controls its subsequent handling.

When you choose to import a JSON backup, Android's file picker lets you select the file. Importing a backup can replace existing locally saved app data after your confirmation. Backups or exports you keep outside the app are not deleted just because you uninstall PrizivoCheck.

Depending on your Android device and Google account settings, system-level device backup services may back up some app data. Such backups are managed by the device/backup provider rather than a PrizivoCheck server. Check your Android backup settings to manage this.

Optional PrizivoCheck Pro purchase

If optional Pro purchasing is offered in your version, it uses Google Play Billing. Google manages payment processing and may process Google account, payment, device and transaction information under Google's own privacy policy. PrizivoCheck does not receive your full payment-card details.

The app may receive and use a product identifier, purchase status, and purchase-verification information supplied by Google Play in order to unlock or restore Pro features. It stores a local last-known Pro entitlement and the time of its last ownership check. The current app does not transmit purchase tokens to a developer-operated verification server. Google Play's own purchase records are controlled by Google.

Permissions, advertising and analytics

The Android release contains internet/network-state permissions and Google Play Billing integration, as well as an Android file-sharing provider. Network access supports platform services such as Google Play Billing; the app does not fetch live retail prices from our servers.

This app version does not include a third-party advertising network or behavioral analytics SDK. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or photo-library permissions for its core features. The Android system picker and share sheet may be opened only when you select a file or sharing action.

Data retention and security

Data kept in app storage remains until you delete it through available features, replace it with an import, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app, subject to Android backup behavior. Deleting a local record does not remove any copies you previously exported or sent to third parties. Pro purchase history maintained by Google is subject to Google's retention practices.

Local data is protected using the device operating system's app storage controls. We do not claim that any device, backup, or transmission is completely secure. Emails you send to our support address are processed by our email provider and retained only as needed to respond, maintain records required by law, or resolve legitimate issues; you may request deletion where applicable.

Children

PrizivoCheck is a general shopping calculation utility and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly solicit children's personal information through the app. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the support address, contact us so we can review the request.

Your choices and rights

You can review your in-app data, remove saved comparison records where supported, control your exported files, and uninstall or clear the app's data. You can contact us at support.velqenor@gmail.com for privacy questions, or for requests to access, correct or delete personal data we may hold through support communications.

Depending on where you live, including in the EEA, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy law (including objection, restriction and complaint to a supervisory authority). Payment/account data processed by Google should be managed through Google's services and privacy controls. Some processing by platform, email or hosting providers may occur outside your country under their own terms.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if app features, services, or legal requirements change. The current effective date is shown above. If a future update adds analytics, ads, cloud sync or developer-operated services, this disclosure will be reviewed before those features are introduced.
